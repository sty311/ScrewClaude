## Natural Earth boundary data

src/china-boundary.json derives from Natural Earth 1:10m map units, public domain. Source revision and SHA-256 are recorded in that file. See https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/. Coordinates are evaluated locally. This simplified dataset is used for approximate detection, not authoritative boundary claims.
